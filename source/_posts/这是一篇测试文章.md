# 2、公式测试


麦克斯韦方程组的积分形式：

<div class="katex-block">
  \left\{
  \begin{array}{l}
  \displaystyle \iint_S \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = Q_f \\[8pt]
  \displaystyle \oint_L \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = -\iint_S \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} \cdot d\mathbf{S} \\[8pt]
  \displaystyle \iint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = 0 \\[8pt]
  \displaystyle \oint_L \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \iint_S \left( \mathbf{J}_f + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t} \right) \cdot d\mathbf{S}
  \end{array}
  \right.
</div>

麦克斯韦方程组的微分形式：
<div class="katex-block">
\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
\displaystyle \nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho_f \\[8pt]
\displaystyle \nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} \\[8pt]
\displaystyle \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0 \\[8pt]
\displaystyle \nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J}_f + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}
\end{array}
\right.
</div>

<div class="katex-block">
R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} R g_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}
</div>
